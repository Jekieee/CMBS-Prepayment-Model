Project Overview

This project evaluates and compares traditional statistical models and machine learning approaches for predicting pool-level prepayment behavior in Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities (CMBS), using two public agency datasets: Fannie Mae DUS and Freddie Mac K-Deal loans.

The objective is to assess differences in predictive performance, stability, and interpretability, with a focus on capturing nonlinear prepayment incentives and borrower behavior.
